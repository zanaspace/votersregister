Why We Audit Politicians' Polling Units

In Nigerian elections, the home polling units of presidents, governors, and party chieftains are high-scrutiny centers. An unnatural voter surge (>25%–50%) in a politician's home ward often signals register padding, while a sudden shrinkage can indicate opposition voter suppression. The alarm below is triggered whenever a VIP polling unit deviates from Nigeria's natural 4-year demographic baseline (12%).
